Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

[App name] ("the app") is a learning app for young children. In short: it collects nothing.

What stays on the device

A parent enters a child's first name and age, and may choose an optional profile photo. The app also records learning progress, rewards earned, screen-time settings and sound preferences. All of this is stored only on the device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. We cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising or advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No network requests while the child plays.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, contacts or location. The optional profile photo comes through the system photo picker, which gives the app only the single photo selected.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 2–5 and is used with a parent. Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, no verifiable parental consent is needed. Settings, the parent dashboard and external links are protected by a parental gate.

Deleting data

Deleting a child in Settings removes their name, progress and photo from the device. Deleting the app removes everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Any future feature that sends data off the device would be disclosed here first and would require parental consent.
